Our story

Great products deserve
a stronger engine.

Too many sellers build great products and then get buried — in spreadsheets, support tickets, ad dashboards and a channel they don't fully control. Revenue grows, but so does the workload, and nobody can say for sure what's actually profitable.

IsaPlus was built to fix that. We bring the pieces together — a brand website that's truly yours, AI that takes the repetitive work off your plate, listings and ads tuned for profit, and reporting that tells the truth.

The globe in our logo is the marketplace. The packages are your products. Our job is making sure they get where they need to go — and keep coming back.

Our mission

Give every Amazon brand we work with the systems of a much larger company — without the overhead.

Our vision

A world where independent sellers own their brand, understand their numbers and grow on their terms.
